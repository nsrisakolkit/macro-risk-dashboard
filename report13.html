The equity segment of our portfolio is strategically positioned to capitalize on current market dynamics, with a focus on growth-oriented assets. The Invesco QQQ Trust (QQQ) and Invesco S&P 500 Momentum ETF (SPMO) have shown robust month-to-date returns of 4.70% and 4.31%, respectively, benefiting from the tech sector's rally and momentum-driven market trends.

Amid expectations of a Federal Reserve rate cut, growth stocks are likely to continue their upward trajectory, supported by easing inflation and increased consumer spending. This environment favors our holdings in QQQ and SPMO, which are well-aligned with sectors sensitive to interest rate changes.

Conversely, the Vanguard Value ETF (VTV), with a 2.83% return, may face challenges if rates decline further, as value stocks typically thrive in rising rate scenarios. However, stable inflation could still provide a supportive backdrop for VTV.

Our exposure to the iShares MSCI China ETF (MCHI) is also noteworthy, with a 4.92% return, reflecting cautious optimism in China's economic recovery efforts. Monitoring MCHI for further recovery signals remains a priority.

In conclusion, our equity holdings are well-positioned to leverage the anticipated growth in the markets, with strategic adjustments to enhance portfolio performance in response to evolving economic indicators.
